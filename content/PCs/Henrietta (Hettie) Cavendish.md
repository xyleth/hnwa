- Runs Once upon a Scone, a local Cafe from which she provides the baked goods for HWNA meetings.
- Has a pet mouse Herbert, who is clearly her Familiar.
- Is obviously a witch
- Aubrey saved Hettie from another Fae once, she was investigating the disappearance of some children who had inadvertently fallen through a Farie circle, leading Hettie to believe the Fae were actively kidnapping children.  She got into an overly kinetic argument with the local Fae and Aubrey intervened and mediated a truce and the return of the children.  This required Aubrey to reveal his nature to Hettie.
- A sorcerer was stealing garden gnomes to build a ritual circle, they stopped him but the backlash of magical power threatened to tip Hettie over the edge, Aubrey managed to talk her back from the precipice.
