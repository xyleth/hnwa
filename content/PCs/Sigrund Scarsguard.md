- Descendent of Vikings
- A mountain of a man
- Does odd jobs for hire, like bouncer 
- Is a Werebear (not sure if Aubrey knows this), meaning he has an aversion to Silver
- He and Aubrey fought against overwhelming odds together.  A old school necromancer was raising the local dead.  They battled against the horde all night and managed to slay the necromancer at the crack of dawn, turning all the undead to mist.  The locals were none the wiser, going about their lives without knowing they'd been breathing granny.
- He once lost control and tried to kill Aubrey.  Aubrey talked him down by using his Fae Glamour powers, turning him into a purring puddle of Bear fur.
