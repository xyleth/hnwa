Here's the updated character concept:

Your character is Major Lord Augustus Fitzroy-Hamilton of the 13th Ethereal Dragoons (nicknamed "The Queen's Specters"), a secret Victorian military unit that combats supernatural threats to the Empire. However, he was born Samuel "Sam" Blackwood, a common soldier.

Core backstory:

- Originally Sam Blackwood, a common soldier in Britain's colonial wars
- Had an uncanny resemblance to Lord Augustus Fitzroy-Hamilton, a noble officer
- Encountered something supernatural that killed the real Lord Fitzroy-Hamilton
- Survived because whatever they encountered mistook him for the noble
- Assumed the officer's identity and returned to England
- Was proactively recruited by the 13th, who had been watching the Fitzroy-Hamilton family for generations as they historically provided officers to the unit
- The 13th recognized the deception but saw value in having someone with both noble authority and real combat experience

Key assets:

- A distinctive monster-hunting carriage equipped with:
    - Hydraulic rotating weapon panels for different supernatural threats
    - Reinforced structure and protective sigils
    - Special lanterns and detection equipment
    - Hidden compartments and emergency supplies
    - Cutting-edge Victorian engineering
- His driver/confidant Thomas "Tom" Willoughby:
    - A stable boy from his past who knows his true identity
    - Now serves as his driver and valet
    - The only person who still calls him "Sam" in private
    - Helps maintain both his cover and his specialized equipment
