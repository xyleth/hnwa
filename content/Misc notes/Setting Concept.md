Good morning all!

I hope you are all keen and eager to get started tonight and join the curtain-twitching nosy neighbours of the Hollywell neighbourhood watch association!

To give you an idea of the general feel of the game, it is meant to be in the style of your classic monster/mystery of the week shows like buffy, supernatural or the x files, but with a midsommer murders twist.

Set in the fictional holywell district of the cotswolds the HNWA makes it their business to stick their noses into any strange goings on in the unlimited sprawl of small hamlets, villages and old manor houses of the region. Are they an official organisation? Do they have any official status? Are they just annoying busybodies and new age hippy nutjobs? Who knows?
