Ashley missing due to ill health.

We pick up where we left off [[2025-04-25]].

We drive up to the point where the car left the road.  Along the road we notice tyre marks consistent with the crashed car, but also heavier tractor tire marks showing that it swerved off the road before making it back.  We find a section of demolished dry stone wall with tyre marks leading through it, a car appears to have travelled at speed through the wall and into the trees beyond.  The tracks thread through the trees without hitting any and show indication that the driver was moving at considerable speed, especially given the terrain.

Hettie detects residual glamour magic that conjured a magical fog and a imaginary road leading the car safely through the woods, at least until its untimely descent into the gorge.

To Aburey the magic feels familiar, Fae in origin, but not from someone/thing he knows.  But nor does it feel out of place.  Aubrey attunes himself to the memory of the place in an attempt to observe the memory of what happened that night.  He sees the car travelling at speed, following a fast moving light.  He gets the sound of small feet moving quickly, a mischievous giggle and a dark, fast moving creature jumping from tree to tree leading the car toward the cliff.  At the cliff edge the mischief turns malicious as the car plummets to its doom, and he feels a shiver of intense cold.

Unfortunately in his focus he veers too close to the edge and is spotted by the DI below, who is not impressed.  She sends Gregg up the path with police tape.

We are close to the Brynmor estate, the Brynmor family are another example of fallen landed gentry.  About a year ago the manor was sold to [[Clive Raynard]], father of [[Bentley Raynard]], but the family themselves still reside at Brynmor farmhouse and work the land.  [[Roderick Brynmor]] is the surviving scion of the family who lives at the farmhouse.  Hettie and Aubrey decide to pay Roderick a visit.

Clive has Roderick building a aviary to house Falcons for his amusement / rich friends.   They talk about the tractor off roading and he recounts that he was evading a oncoming car, which he identified as 'that Reynard boy'.  Aubrey attempts to lure him into disclosing some malice or animus towards Bently.  Obviously he is no fan of the Reynards, and he is not truly as upset as he makes out, but there is no sense that he expected or planned this.

We next decide to return to the village hall and speak with [[Millicent Brompton]], the local busybody, to try and determine if there is a specific motive behind this or if it was a random attack.  Millicent is found out the back of the hall putting some saucers of milk down on the ground, allegedly for Hedgehogs - 'One for the hedgehog one for the Puck', the Puck being a 'old wives tale'.  Unsurprisingly she already knows Bently is dead.  This is an exciting moment for Millicent, she is terribly xenophobic to anyone from outside the village and relishes the thought of the gossip to come about the young mans death.

Hettie remembers about 'Pookas', a variant of Pucks that are helpful to those who respect them but mischievously malicious to those who don't - however they've never been known to straight up murder folk.  They are animal shifters, often into horses.

Having gotten not a lot from Millicent we decide to visit the Stables at the Manor to talk to [[Porsche Raynard]].  We find Porsche and Clive in the courtyard, Porsche is overseeing a quartet of teens leading horses from the stables onto the weekly trek.  She has been crying and Clive is comforting her.

They try to talk to Clive, it does not go well and he storms off.  Hettie gets some information from Porsche, revealing that Bently has had run of bad luck with his car.  Losing his keys, a jack failing while he was under it tinkering.  Porsche herself has found items going missing, her bridle this morning.  It transpires that a new horse was delivered that morning, that she thinks she was expecting.  Aubrey and Hettie move to investigate the horse (Aubrey rolls the only 12 of the night!).  Hettie gets a 10 and gets Hold 3 on Read a Bad Situation:

Spend 1: 'Are there any dangers we haven't noticed': The boy is wearing a name tag 'Puck'.
Spend 2: 'What's the best way to protect the victims': Puck and the new horse are at the front of the group and could easily be isolated, but there are four other horses that may startle at loud action.

Aubrey tries to put the Whammy on Porsche to get her to shut the day down, but he fails the roll.  He does complete his experience meter so gets to level up!  Porsche clears her head and declares the show must go on, taking her bridle from Puck.  Cut to ad break!
