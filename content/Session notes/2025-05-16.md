Everyone here!

We pick up where we [[2025-05-07]] left off, except with the minor retcon of Aubrey spending a Luck point to command [[Porsche Raynard]] to shut the class up and go indoors.  This costs him and he needs to feed so he bigs it up in front of the other riders to capture some of that sweet, sweet human attention.  This distracts him as he leads them toward the stables, leaving Hettine and Sigrund to deal with Puck.

The glamour horse dissipates.  Sigrund returns to the courtyard with the Puck by the scruff of the neck and a 'discussion' entails between Aubrey and his 'cousin'.

The Puck has been targeting the family because they haven't been 'paying their dues' and the humans are 'disrupting the natural order of things'.  When challenged by Aubrey that this will lead to a dark place with the humans doing more damage to the Fae, to which Puck responds 'perhaps they already are' and that 'the horns are sounding in the forest'.

This is vaguely familiar to Aubrey but not immediately relevant, so he files it for future reference and moves on.  Puck tries to escape but Sigrund easily restrains him.  Aubrey makes his situation clear and offers Puck a deal - in return for his freedom Puck will leave the Raynard's alone for one week while the party investigate what is happening and attempt to find a mutually agreeable solution that can resolve the conflict without further bloodshed.

Puck tries to bargain for payment, but Aubrey counters that his freedom is payment enough.  Puck sees which way this is going and agrees, exchanging handshakes with Aubrey.  They both take advantage of the 'shake to pluck a hair from the others arm so as to have something to hold over them (Aubrey rolled a partial success so Puck got a hair too).

The deal sealed Aubrey signals Sigrund to release Puck who reverts to his rabbit form and leaps over the roof, unleashing a torrent of backed up rainwater over Hettie.

They have resolved the immediate mystery and prevented further harm to innocents, ending the episode.  The party gain two experience and we go to the post credits scheme - Aubrey researching the 'horns'.

The Horns are strongly linked with the concept of the Wyld Hunt.  Two things stick out from his conversations with family members;
1) Aubrey's family may have obligations to the hunt should it be called
2) If the Lady of the Hunt is stirring in the Summer this is not a good sign, we will be in for a 'harsh winter'

A hunt has not been called for some time.

We leave it there for night, ready for the cold open of Ep 2 on May 28 th.
