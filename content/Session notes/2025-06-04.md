A little later than scheduled we are back together for Episode 2 - Fair play.

Aubrey has been given the keys to the sealed wing of the family pile, discovering an extensive library and fully stocked armoury.  That this came from the family elders in response to his investigations around the 'Horns' and 'Hunt' is not lost on him.

Sigrund has developed an animal like attractivness.

We open a couple of days after the last episode ends with a woman late at night finding a body and then a mysterious stranger appearing in reflection behind her.  There is a scream which cuts off abruptly and the camera pans to the empty street, the woman vanished and mysterious figure with her leaving only the body in a high vis jacket on the ground.  Roll credits.

After credits we find Sigrund in [[The Spindle]], Hettie is running her tea shop on the Saturday afternoon and Aubrey is returning from his visit to family in the 'back country' somewhere remote in Wales.

Sigrund is approached by [[Amelia Brook]], a local girl, who is looking for his help.  Her aunt [[Celia Jones]] has not arrived for a promised appointment to make pancakes for breakfast.  This is very out of character for the Aunt so she has sought Sigrund out as a known member if HNWA, he agrees to look for her and informs the group via message.

Aubrey sets out to the [[The Handmaid]], one of Celia's know haunts (the other being the Spindle where Sigrund is).  He catches up with the manager [[Summer Baxter]] and establishes that Celia was, indeed, in the previous night with some friends and had a good night.  After some (many) drinks there they caught wind of a lock in at the Spindle and left for that pub.  Aubrey and Summer make chit chat and agree to meet later at the fair for a drink.

We shift now to Sigrund at the Spindle where he gets talking to Brigette, one of the owners.  He establishes that Celia did make it to the pub that night and suggested a lock-in to Alex (who always had a soft spot for her).  They left around 02:00, noted to be 'howling like drunk foxes'.  Brigette is not a fan and somewhat judgy.  She saw her stagger away across the green along the fence erected for the fair.

Everyone meets outside the Spindle in one of those moments so serendipitous it could have been scripted.  Hettie has a bad feeling about what may have happened, and apparently talks like an Italian plumbers sidekick now.  The group decides to re-trace her steps across the green towards the Brook family house on the assumption that is where Celia would have gone.  The path leads around the back of the fairground so the area is less travelled but still busy at this time.  The grass here is longer and less well manicured than the main green, enough to show an indentation roughly human sized where something/one has clearly lain for some time.  Opposite a gap has been opened in the metal fencing to the fair just large enough for someone to squeeze through.

Sigrund gets his sniff on, the area reeks of booze but there is an undercurrent of Celia's scent, the trail ends at this point.  A strong smell of body odour and stale alcohol linger in the impression in the grass - these do not smell like Celia.  Next to this indentation there are two footprints that don't go anywhere, or appear to have come from anywhere, appear to be too long for human feet and they have no scent.  The gap in the fence leads around the back of Mo's Kebab van.

They go in via the front entrance, paying their way, and make their way back to the kebab van.  Hettie feels for magic and finds a trace of old binding magic laid over the entire field.  She discovers a torn scrap of high viz jacket caught on a tine of the fence.

At that moment John interrupts them with a hand on Aubrey's shoulder.  He tells an embarrassing story about Aubrey's youth and confirms that Celia is due to visit that evening before heading off back to the fair.  He throws on a high viz that has a suspicious tear matching where one may have been on the gap in the fence.  They press him on when he last saw Celia and he reveals that after one too many drinks he took a 'nap' behind the fare, explaining the dent in the grass, and he says he swears he saw Celia standing over him and then blinked and she was gone, but he puts that down to the booze.

Sigrund's sense of smell matches the smell of John to the smell of the indentation earlier.

Aubrey heads into the crowds to 'feed' at the side shows, using minor magic to make himself the centre of attention and soak up the admiration.  While he is juggling the balls before theatrically throwing them into buckets to win prizes, to many adoring ooohhs and ahhhs, Hettie uses the distraction to engage one of the staff in conversation.

Hettie finds on the back of one of the stands a small saucer with some dregs of milk in it and a Puck sat next to it.  The Puck interferes with Aubrey's ball games, turning a slam dunk into a near miss. Someone is clearly honouring the old ways and the fae are in residence.  Some of the carvings in the decretive woodwork of the stands is celtic in nature and relates to binding magic.

Sigrund is investigating clown feet to see if they match our mystery footprints.  The clowns, for there are multiple of them, on closer inspection are identical. Same height, same clothes, same makeup, everything.  Their feet do match the indents.  They do not seem to move in the normal sense.  Sure they turn up in different places, but at no point does he see them actually move between spaces.  And weirdest of all - they have no scent.  One of them beckons him over and blows a balloon animal for him, a teddy bear (remember Sigrund is a were-bear).  He passes it over and for a moment it feels cold and hard, like porcelain, before suddenly becoming properly balloon like as the clown releases it.
