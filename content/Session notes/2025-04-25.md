We do session 0 and get to know one another.  See [[Relationship to other players]]

We then start with a scene of a brand new, bright red Audi speeding through the villages chasing a impossible motor biker before plummeting into Honeywell gorge.

[[Millicent Brompton]] - Village hall warden.  Sigrund overhears her discussing this with her son, a traffic cop.  He calls Hettie and Aubrey to inform them of this happening.  They agree to meet at Once upon a Scone and go from there.  Aubrey picks them up in his battered Land Rover Defender and drives them up to the gorge car park and hike up the trail to the scene.

They arrive on the scene to find [[Gregg Brompton]], Millicent's son, guarding some hastily placed police tape.  He is accompanied by [[DI Rebecca Crow ]]in forensic boots and gloves, a chain smoking detective who is not a fan of HNWA.  The conversation does not go well and DI Crow basically tells them to piss off.  Aubrey does not feel like messing with the minds of the local constabulary first thing in the morning so Hettie comes up with a alternative solution: she remembers an old miners path along the bottom of the gorge to get to the same point without being observed by the police.  They make it along the path big Sigrund turns his ankle, Aubrey and Hettie look disheveled.

Sigurd and Hettie set to investigating while Aubrey tries and fails to keep watch for an incoming forensics team.  Hettie discovers that Glamour magic was used and recognises the driver as [[Bentley Raynard]] .  Out of time due to the impending arrival of the forensic squad they decide to retreat and go and investigate the area where the car left the cliff.

We leave it with the party back at the car park at Aubrey's car.
