The Brynmor family are another example of fallen landed gentry.  About a year ago the manor was sold to [[Clive Raynard]], father of [[Bentley Raynard]], but the family themselves still reside at Brynmor farmhouse and work the land.  [[Roderick Brynmor]] is the surviving scion of the family who lives at the farmhouse.
