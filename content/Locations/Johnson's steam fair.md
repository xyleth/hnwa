Johnson's Steam fair is a Hollywell Borough institution. Old Jimmy Johnson started with one steam caravan and built up to a full travelling carnival. His son Jimbo inherited the fair, as did his son James and most recently the fair has been inherited by his son and stepson Jim and John. 

Holywell locals will have been to the fair growing up and will know the Johnsons by sight at least and a good few will know them better. Everyone knows James' son Jim works hard to keep the engines running and the books balanced, his stepson John is the life and soul of the show and can always be found delighting the fairgoers with a story or sharing a pint with the locals in the nearest pub, maybe one or two pints too many if truth be told.

Rosie Johnson, the second wife is a sour-faced crone everyone knows to give her caravan a wide berth or face a dressing down for something.

The major attractions / areas are:

- Ghost train
- Carousel
- Hall of Mirrors
- Haunted house
- Food court
- Side shows (games)
- Steam engines
- Caravans / living quarters
